How life changed before and after the reform and opening up

China's transformation from a relatively poor country into one of the world's most important economic powers was nothing short of remarkable. In 1978, the Chinese leader Deng Xiaoping began the policy of "Reform and Opening Up" (改革開放). He introduced policies that reshaped China and fuelled the nation's rise. We've all heard about how these reforms changed China, but how has everyday life actually changed for Chinese people?
